About.

Vaishanavi Saware, biotechnologist. Clinical operations at Genentech, after a flow cytometry core and a product team.

See the work

I am a Carnegie Mellon biotechnology graduate with three years of experience across industry and academia: scientific research, clinical operations, and product marketing management, at Gladstone Institutes, Genentech and Promega.

The thread through all three is the same: translating between the people who generate data and the people who have to decide something with it. In a flow cytometry core that meant other labs’ panels. At Promega it meant the teams selling what the scientists had built. At Genentech it means study teams, and the systems of record that have to agree with each other.

Before any of that I was at UC Davis, reading neurobiology, physiology and behavioural sciences with a pre-medical focus, with time in the Ho Lab and at the veterinary teaching hospital.

The work since has run in three strands, and each has a page: research, product, operations.
