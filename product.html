Four months in the summer of 2025, as a product management intern on the Genomics and Instruments team at Promega, in Madison, Wisconsin.

The short version: accelerated sales readiness, amplified genomics brand reach, and enabled scalable UX and executive decision making, through first of its kind content, influencer strategy, and analytics.

Promega’s genomics customers are the labs I came from: core facilities, academic groups and biotech R&D teams buying reagents and the instruments that run them. Selling to a scientist who will run the thing herself is a different problem from selling to a procurement line, and it is the problem this team works on.
