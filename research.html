Four of the nine roles here are research roles. The longest was Gladstone Institutes, where one year and ten months ran across two grades of research associate, much of it in the flow cytometry core.

A flow cytometry core is shared instrumentation. The machines are booked by labs from across an institute, and the people who run one keep them calibrated, help design the panels that go on them, and make sure the numbers coming off mean the same thing in March as they did in January.

The graduate research was at Carnegie Mellon, in two labs across two colleges: the Cai Lab in the Mellon College of Science through the first half of 2025, and the Robinson Lab in the College of Engineering through the second, on mammalian cell culture and cell line development. Before all of it, five months as an undergraduate in the Ho Lab at UC Davis.
